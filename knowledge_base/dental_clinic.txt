SmileCare Dental Clinic — Lahore
Clinic Overview

SmileCare Dental Clinic is a modern, patient-centered dental practice located on MM Alam Road, Gulberg III, Lahore. Established in 2015, the clinic serves more than 5,000 patients annually with a team of six specialized dentists, state-of-the-art digital imaging, and strict international sterilization protocols. The clinic is conveniently located just 5 minutes from Liberty Market and 10 minutes from DHA Phase 3.

Emergency Dental Care
Call the Emergency Line If You Experience:
Severe toothache accompanied by swelling.
A knocked-out tooth.
Continuous bleeding that does not stop.
Jaw injury or fracture.
Dental abscess with fever.
Emergency Response Process
Call 0300-1234567 (Available 24/7).
Dr. Imran Malik or the on-call dentist will respond.
