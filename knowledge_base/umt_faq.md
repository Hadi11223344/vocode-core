# University of Management and Technology (UMT) — Admission & Campus Guide

## About UMT

The University of Management and Technology (UMT) is a top-tier private university in Lahore, Pakistan, chartered by the Government of Punjab and recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan. Located in the Johar Town area near Khayaban-e-Jinnah, UMT spans a modern 40-acre campus with state-of-the-art labs, a digital library, sports complexes, and dedicated hostels for male and female students.

Established in 1990, UMT has grown into one of Pakistan's most respected institutions, ranked among the top 10 universities nationally by HEC. The university emphasizes industry linkage, entrepreneurship, and research-driven education.

## Graduate Programs

- Duration: 2 years (4 semesters) or 1.5 years for BBA graduates
- Eligibility: 16 years of education, minimum 2.5 CGPA or 50% marks
- Total Fee: PKR 1,200,000

- GMAT/GAT not required for UMT graduates

### MS Computer Science
- Duration: 2 years
- Eligibility: BS CS/SE/IT with minimum 2.5 CGPA
- Total Fee: PKR 800,000
- Research areas: AI, Blockchain, IoT, Cloud Computing

### MS Data Science
- Duration: 2 years
- Eligibility: Relevant bachelor's with 2.5 CGPA
- Total Fee: PKR 900,000

### MS Electrical Engineering
- Duration: 2 years
- Eligibility: BS Electrical Engineering with 2.5 CGPA
- Total Fee: PKR 950,000

## Admission Process

### Step-by-Step
1. **Online Application**: Apply at admissions.umt.edu.pk
2. **Document Submission**: Matric, Inter, CNIC/B-Form, 2 photos
3. **Admissions Test (UAT)**: Computer-based at UMT campus or approved centers
4. **Interview**: For select programs (BBA, MBA, PhD)
5. **Merit List**: Published within 7 days of test
6. **Fee Submission**: Within 15 days of admission offer

### Important Dates (Typical Academic Year)
- Spring Intake: Applications open November, classes start February
- Fall Intake: Applications open June, classes start September
- Late applications accepted with PKR 5,000 surcharge

### Merit Formula
- Intermediate marks: 60% weight
- Entry test (UAT): 30% weight
- Interview (if applicable): 10% weight

## Scholarships & Financial Aid

### Merit Scholarships
| Criteria | Discount |
|----------|----------|

| 85-89% | 75% tuition waiver |
| 80-84% | 50% tuition waiver |

### HEC Need-Based Scholarship
- For families with income below PKR 50,000/month
- Covers full tuition + monthly stipend PKR 6,000
- Requires income certificate from Union Council

### UMT Alumni Scholarship
- 20% discount for children of UMT alumni
- Valid for all undergraduate programs

### Sports Scholarship
- 25-100% waiver for national/provincial level athletes
- Requires sports certificate and trial

### Special Category Scholarships
- Disabled students: 50% waiver
- Martyrs' children (Army/Police): 100% waiver
- Gilgit-Baltistan students: 50% waiver

- On-campus jobs (library, labs, admin) for deserving students
- PKR 15,000-25,000/month, 20 hours/week
