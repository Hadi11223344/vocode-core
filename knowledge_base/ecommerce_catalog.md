# StyleHub.pk — Online Fashion Store Catalog

## Store Overview

StyleHub.pk is a Lahore-based online fashion retailer specializing in Pakistani ethnic wear, Western casuals, and fusion outfits for men, women, and children. Founded in 2018, the store serves customers across Pakistan with delivery in 2-3 days for Lahore and 5-7 days for other cities. All products are sourced from local manufacturers in Faisalabad, Karachi, and Sialkot.

## Delivery & Policies

### Delivery Areas
- Lahore: 2-3 business days (same-day available for PKR 500 extra)
- Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi: 3-5 business days
- Other cities: 5-7 business days
- Cash on Delivery (COD) available for orders under PKR 15,000
- Online payment: JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer, Credit/Debit Card

### Return & Exchange
- 7-day exchange for size issues (product must be unworn with tags)
- No returns on sale/discounted items
- Exchange process: WhatsApp photo of issue → approval → courier pickup → replacement sent
- Return shipping: Customer pays PKR 250 for pickup

### Order Tracking
- Tracking number sent via SMS/WhatsApp within 24 hours
- Track at www.stylehub.pk/track
- Customer service available 9 AM - 9 PM daily
