# SmileCare Dental Clinic — Lahore

## Clinic Overview

SmileCare Dental Clinic is a modern, patient-centered dental practice located on MM Alam Road, Gulberg III, Lahore. Established in 2015, the clinic serves over 5,000 patients annually with a team of 6 specialized dentists, state-of-the-art digital imaging, and strict international sterilization protocols. The clinic is easily accessible — 5 minutes from Liberty Market and 10 minutes from DHA Phase 3.

## Emergency Dental Care

### When to Call Emergency Line
- Severe toothache with swelling
- Knocked-out tooth
- Bleeding that won't stop
- Jaw injury or fracture
- Abscess with fever

### Emergency Response
- Call 0300-1234567 (24/7)
- Dr. Imran Malik or on-call dentist available
- Severe cases referred to Services Hospital or Shaukat Khanum if needed
- First-aid guidance provided over phone
